# Briefly summarize the DriverPass project. Who was the client? What type of system did they want you to design?
The main project for thsi course was designing a systme for our client DriverPass, a company who wished to 
to aid new drivers, by teaching them and expanding their experience on the road. They wanted their system to be a web-based
platform, that would allow their users to access from anywhere to study or schedule driving practice. 

# What did you do particularly well?
I think for this project, I did a good job at creating easy to follow and clean diagrams, that showcased the systems design.
In particular, I think I did a good job with the UML case diagram, by showing the different actors involved and the various use cases
that would be needed in the system.

# If you could choose one part of your work on these documents to revise, what would you pick? How would you improve it?
I think I would revise some portions of the requirements document, I felt that I may have not included certain functionalities in it, that I could 
have included. I think by doing this, I would have had an overall better requirements document, that would have helped me then create better diagrams for
the later project.

# How did you interpret the user’s needs and implement them into your system design? Why is it so important to consider the user’s needs when designing?
To interpret the user's needs, I imagined myself using the program and what aspects I would like to see in. I think this is important because, as someone who
may not be a user, the program will eventually be used by many others, who would like to have a working and easy to use application. This in turn would give them reasons
to recommend it to others.  

# How do you approach designing software? What techniques or strategies would you use in the future to analyze and design a system?
 I think when it comes to designing software, it is important to one make sure it is adaptable, so that it can later b updated or improved. And 
 secondly to have the thought of the end users in mind. In the future, I think I would implement the sue of surveys or interviews to get an idea of
 what other people would like out of a system. I would also make sure to utilize various diagrams and models to showcase the system in a graphical way.
